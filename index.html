The House of Káráyá

Where luxury meets tranquility.

Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know before your visit. If your question isn't here, our team is one message away.

Still wondering about something? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply as soon as we can.

How do I book an appointment?

The quickest way is WhatsApp. Tap any Reserve button on this page — or message 0810 380 8549 — and tell us which treatment you would like. Our team will reply with available dates and times. You can also call the same number on 0810 380 8549.

Which treatments do you offer?

Our menu includes massage therapy (Swedish, deep tissue, sport, hot stone, prenatal, ashiatsu and aromatherapy), facials, body scrubs, manicure and pedicure, waxing for women and men, IV wellness drips, and our signature spa packages. Every treatment is listed with its price in the menu above.

How much do your treatments cost?

Every treatment on this page is listed with its current price in Nigerian Naira. Waxing starts from ₦5,000, a manicure is ₦15,000, massages start at ₦60,000, and our wellness and spa packages range from ₦70,500 to ₦235,000. Prices are confirmed again when you book.

What are your opening hours?

Please message us on WhatsApp and our team will confirm today's availability and opening hours before you travel.
